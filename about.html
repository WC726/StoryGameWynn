Deeply amateur coder and longtime Dungeons & Dragons aficionado, screaming-trash-oppossum has managed to take a break from writing their dissertation on the influence of the developement of a extrasolar system's home star on its ability to host Earth-like planets long enough to create this mini horror game. A lover of the gory and unsettling, they were inspired to creat this game simply on the basis that they like making creepy and unsettling stuff.

When not staring down evil and onimous job applications, they enjoy watching anime, playing D&D, waking up at 5:30 am 6 days a week to engage in physical exercise, attempting to assist their husband with cosplay-making, taking amateur photographs, and making even more amateur DIY accessories.

Their advice for any young people is, "Keep calm and always carry at least 3 more knives than anyone expects you to have." They also strongly recommend the Discworld series by Terry Pratchett to anyone looking for their next read.

screaming-trash-oppossum lives inside your walls with their husband, niece, and two adoptive daughters.
